Creating Awareness About Waste Management

Proper garbage management is the backbone of a healthy, sustainable city. By understanding our impact and making small daily changes, we can reduce pollution, prevent disease, and protect our environment for future generations.

🗑️

Reduce Waste

Buy only what you need. Minimizing daily garbage generation is the first step to a cleaner city.

♻️

Segregate Waste

Always keep dry and wet waste separate at the source to make recycling and composting easier.

🚫

Avoid Plastic

Say no to single-use plastics. Switch to reusable bags, bottles, and containers today.

🤝

Community Action

Take ownership of your neighborhood. Encourage others and participate in local clean drives.
